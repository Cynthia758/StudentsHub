FAQs

How Can We Help You?

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a centralized portal where students can access announcements, events, academic updates and profile information.

How do I register?

Click on the Register button from the navigation bar, fill in your details and create your account.

Can I register for events online?

Yes. Every event page includes a Register button allowing students to participate easily.

How can I update my profile?

After logging in, open the Profile page from your dashboard to edit your information.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Click on the "Forgot Password?" option on the Login page and follow the instructions.

How do I contact the college?

Visit the Contact page to find phone numbers, email addresses and office location.
